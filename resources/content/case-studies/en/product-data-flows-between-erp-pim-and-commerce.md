This draft should document a field I know well: product data only becomes valuable when it moves cleanly between business systems, sites, marketing catalogs, and search surfaces.

## Angle

A connector is never just a pipe. It is often where catalog quality, team trust, release stability, and SEO readability are won or lost.

## Points to develop

- ERP, PIM, stock, and exports as one living system
- Hydrating product data on the commerce side
- Effects on Merchant Center, Meta Catalog, and structured data
- Trade-offs between quick wins and deeper debt

## What this case study should show

The real problem is not merely syncing fields. It is making product data reliable, understandable, and usable for business teams, interfaces, and robots.
