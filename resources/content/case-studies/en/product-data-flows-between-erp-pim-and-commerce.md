This case study sits on a familiar problem: product data only becomes valuable when it moves cleanly between business systems, commerce surfaces, and search or marketing feeds.

## Situation

A connector between two systems looks like a pipe. In practice it is where you find out whether the catalog is right, whether the teams trust what they see, and whether a release breaks something.

## What matters here

- ERP, PIM, stock, and exports as one living system
- Hydrating product data on the commerce side
- Effects on Merchant Center, Meta Catalog, and structured data
- Trade-offs between quick wins and deeper debt

## Expected outcome

The real problem is not merely syncing fields. It is making product data reliable, understandable, and usable for business teams, interfaces, and robots.
