`YTMusic Liked Sorter` came from a simple need: take back a history, sort it, and recover things that had disappeared into volume.

The public repository lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/Ismaouste/ytmusic-liked-sorter). The point is precisely to keep the interface light, the retrieval and filtering logic readable, and the output immediately useful.

I like this kind of note because it shows a calmer relation to code. Not a large promise, just a clean tool that genuinely helps.
