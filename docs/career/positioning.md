# Positioning

## One-line version

Full-stack e-commerce developer focused on Laravel, technical SEO, privacy-safe analytics, and gradual modernization of existing systems.

## Recruiter version

I am most useful on products that already exist, already matter, and need steadier engineering. That usually means Laravel or PHP systems with real business pressure, technical debt, SEO constraints, business integrations, and a need for clearer execution rather than another dramatic rebuild.

## Hiring-manager version

My value is usually at the intersection of implementation and system judgment:

- clean up a legacy surface without freezing delivery
- improve maintainability while respecting operational constraints
- connect technical SEO, routing, metadata, and content structure to product work
- keep privacy and analytics decisions explicit instead of bolting them on later

## What differentiates the profile

- strong fit for e-commerce and other production-sensitive web systems
- comfortable with architecture, implementation, and documentation in the same loop
- practical privacy and consent awareness, not just generic frontend polish
- able to make portfolio and internal work look deliberate, documented, and reusable

## What not to pitch

- greenfield-only experimentation
- vague innovation roles with no delivery ownership
- broad CMS administration or content-marketing execution
- heavy stakeholder hand-holding without clear technical scope
