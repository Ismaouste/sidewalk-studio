# Role Targets

## E-commerce tech lead

Best fit when the company needs a lead who can improve delivery confidence on an existing platform.

What to emphasize:

- production stability
- legacy recovery
- checkout, catalog, tracking, and SEO sensitivity
- practical modernization without platform theater

## Laravel lead developer

Best fit when the system already has history and the team needs stronger boundaries, conventions, and maintainability.

What to emphasize:

- Laravel as a durable delivery layer
- readable architecture and documentation
- incremental refactors
- typed runtime boundaries such as content models or site settings

## Product-minded full-stack engineer

Best fit when the role sits between engineering quality and user-facing clarity.

What to emphasize:

- metadata, routing, and content as product concerns
- consent and privacy inside implementation
- ability to ship both UI polish and backend structure
- comfort with specs, release discipline, and operational docs
